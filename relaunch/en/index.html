01 · Starting point

Why AI in mid-market companies stops after the pilot

We arrive at companies that have been experimenting for a while. What is missing is rarely another tool – it is ownership, evidence and a process that produces a number.

Licences without a metric

ChatGPT, Copilot and Cursor are paid for, but nobody can say how many hours they remove. Without a metric, the investment cannot be defended in the P&L.

Training is no longer optional

Since 2 February 2025, Article 4 of the EU AI Act requires commercial users to ensure AI literacy in their workforce – depending on role and use case, and in a verifiable way.

IT has no capacity for it

Classic software projects run 9 to 18 months. Nobody has capacity for an internal helper tool, while departments lose hours to copy-and-paste every day.

07 · Questions

Questions we get in first calls

What does Vibe Coding mean in an ERP context?

Specialists in purchasing, sales or work preparation describe in plain language what the tool should do. The model writes the code, and the specialist checks the result against real data. Typical outputs are BOM calculators, e-invoice (ZUGFeRD) validators or reorder portals. Anyone who has configured an ERP form before will be comfortable with it.

How does a self-built tool reach ERP data without endangering the system?

Through a three-stage staging layer with schema validation and OData or REST interfaces. Tools only read; there are no write operations on production tables. That is the first question we settle with your IT, not the last.

Is a training certificate enough for Article 4 of the EU AI Act?

Article 4 requires an appropriate level of AI literacy in the workforce, depending on prior knowledge, role and use case. A certificate on its own does not satisfy that. It becomes verifiable through role-specific training content, a usage policy and a register of the systems in use.

Does this work without a US cloud?

Yes. Open-weight models such as Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral or Gemma run through vLLM or Ollama on your own hardware, with the knowledge base in a local vector store such as Qdrant or pgvector. Traffic stays inside the corporate network; a 14 to 32 billion parameter model generally fits on a single GPU.

How quickly does it pay back?

In our projects, payback typically lands at two to four months, calculated with 4.5 to 7 hours saved per week and specialist at €75 per hour. That is an observed figure, not a guarantee: the Opportunity Scan recalculates it with your process times and your hourly rate before you commit.
